This map is for the complete [adopted contract](M8_GILT_CONTRACT.md), not an
expansion of the earlier [52-family proposal](M8_GILT_TEST_DESIGN.md) into
unsupported API shapes. The [validation record](M8_GILT_VALIDATION.md)
provides run identities and remaining acceptance gates. A mapped test is
finite evidence, not a proof that every input in a requirement was executed.

## Limits retained by the map

Twenty-two ideal rows have176 uninformative metric cells and no financial
accuracy assertion. Their typed operational failures remain visible in the
consumer ledger. Finite mathematical corpora, mutation checks and production
region coverage do not prove exhaustive inputs, branch completeness or a libm
theorem. Exact primitive observations are not automatically publicly reachable
yield observations. Foreign native runtime, owner/financial review, legal cash
policy and wider milestone acceptance remain separate.
