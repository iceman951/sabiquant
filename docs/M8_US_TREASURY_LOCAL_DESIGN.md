# Complete Treasury local design proposal

Recorded 2026-10-20 on `feat/m8-us-treasury` from `fc3f629`. These are root's
selected directions for the next **complete** fixed-principal Treasury
tranche, under the owner's continuing local-development authority. They are
review inputs, not an adopted executable contract or a claim that the types
below exist. General numerical fixtures must follow the completed contract
and precede production. No QuantLib implementation was read.

Evidence: [source refresh](M8_US_TREASURY_SOURCE_REFRESH.md),
[printed examples](M8_US_TREASURY_SOURCE_EXAMPLES.md), and
[outward-power research](M8_US_TREASURY_POWER_DESIGN_REVIEW.md). Mathematical
and local convention choices below do not certify actual auctions, legal
cash, secondary-market practice or owner financial acceptance.

## D1. Deliver the complete model together

Implement source/date preparation, price, bounded yield inversion, all six
risk views and all four exact-money recipes together. Keep generic bonds,
gilt contracts, old generators and fixtures unchanged. Use a private Treasury
module exported through `bonds`; no new dependency, unsafe code, I/O, hidden
calendar or clock. Redemption is fixed at par 100. Annual coupon `C` is exact
nonnegative `DecimalAmount` per 100; yield `y` is annual decimal. Preserve the
full nonnegative coupon domain internally, independently of whether an
optional public decimal view fits.

The contemplated public family parallels the complete gilt family:
`UsTreasury`, `TreasuryFirstPeriod`, `TreasuryPricingPlan`,
`TreasuryValuation`, `TreasuryYieldReport`, `TreasuryRisk`, `TreasuryError`,
`TreasuryYieldError`, `TreasuryCashInstruction`, `TreasuryCashDecision` and
`TreasuryCashReceipt`. Separate source-policy types below are additional;
the final census, signatures, traits, error variants and precedence must be
enumerated in the contract. No broad rename or generic pricing abstraction
is needed to implement this model.

## D2. Keep all date roles and the original lattice

Retain original dated date `D`, original issue date `I`, first actual coupon
date `F`, maturity `M`, explicit complete quasi-date vector `Q`, and explicit
`EndOfMonth`. Proposed constructor inequalities are `D <= I < F <= M`, with
the source's accrual coverage determined from **D**, not I. The date
inequalities are a scoped local instrument domain, not a theorem about every
possible announcement.

The seven examples distinguish D from I in the regular case. They do not
discriminate short/long original periods with D<I. Although §§356.2/.30
identify dated-date accrual, I.A.3/4 and II.F literally refer to issue/original
issue dates in their formulas. Extending the short/long coefficients from D
when D<I is an explicit local interpretation, subject to announcement terms,
not a further directly demonstrated literature case.

Require minimal dated coverage `Q[0] <= D < Q[1]`, `Q.last() == M`, strictly
increasing dates, and direct six-month multiples from original M under the
supplied EOM rule. Do not recursively clamp month arithmetic. Classify:

- Regular: `D == Q[0]` and `F == Q[1]`.
- Short: `Q[0] < D < Q[1]` and `F == Q[1]`.
- Long: `Q[0] < D < Q[1]` and `F == Q[2]`.

Actual coupons form the Q suffix from F through M. The long-first skipped
quasi date remains part of the pricing coordinate and has no actual payment.
No irregular final period or special year-long first period is silently
admitted. Source II.D is an important discriminator: its original dated date
is on the regular lattice even though its original issue date is later.

Prepare a settlement `I <= S < M`. This proposal retains every actual coupon
strictly after S and excludes a coupon exactly on S; principal is at M.
This explicit local future-payment convention supplies no investor-level
record-date/ex-coupon assurance. A separate generic bond can model supplied
entitlement conventions. No Federal Reserve closure calendar or payment roll
is generated by the Treasury analytical instrument. Nominal analytical dates
are retained even where actual remittance would be delayed under §356.30.

Let `Q[k]` be strictly after S, `r = days(S,Q[k])`,
`s = days(Q[k-1],Q[k])`, and `n = Q.len()-1-k`. Thus `1 <= r <= s`, and the
retained original quasi index of a bundle is `k+j`. Missing long-first coupons
do not renumber j. The proposed direct semiannual cycle has `181 <= s <= 184`;
the final date proof must verify every admitted EOM/date boundary.

## D3. Separate raw analytics from source cash staging

Raw coupons use exact rational day fractions. Put `c=C/2`:
regular coupon `c`, short first `c*days(D,F)/days(Q[0],F)`, long first
`c*(days(D,Q[1])/days(Q[0],Q[1]) + 1)`. Add principal 100 to the last bundle
exactly. Do not quantize a raw analytical coupon through a public decimal
getter or source daily table.

Raw accrued interest is the sum of exact coupon fractions from D to S before
F, or from the most recent actual coupon to S afterward, split at quasi
boundaries. On an actual coupon date it resets to zero. In the second piece
of a long first period it includes the original fractional piece plus the
elapsed part of the current half-year. Retain that rational independently
of all published observations.

Make source staging explicit with an immutable `TreasurySourcePolicy`, with
no default. It selects a daily basis and a normal-rounding tie rule:

- `TreasuryDailyBasis::ExactRatio` uses `5*C/s` per $1,000 exactly.
- `TreasuryDailyBasis::NineDecimal` rounds that daily ratio to nine places
  before multiplying each fractional day count, reproducing Table 2 staging.
- `TreasuryTieRule::{NearestTiesEven, NearestTiesAwayFromZero}` selects ties
  explicitly; neither is asserted to be the unspecified regulatory tie rule.

Both policies retain full regular coupons `C/2`; multiplying a rounded daily
decimal by a full half-year is only a research control. Long first coupon
cash combines its fractional piece and the full coupon before scaling.
For accrued cash, sum the selected-basis per-$1,000 pieces **before** rounding
the total to five places, then scale nominal. Component rounding is a
different recipe and is not silently substituted. General C outside the
source table's 1/8%-increment range is a named mathematical extension.

Retain both raw AI and published AI. Proposed analytical clean quotes use
`Dhat - cached_published_AI`, with one exact binary reduction, rather than
raw AI. The published value is the chosen daily basis followed by total
five-place per-$1,000 rounding, equivalent to six places per 100. A separate
`raw_accrued_per_100` observation makes the difference inspectable. Dirty
price and risk do not require a clean-price getter.

The pricing kernel itself does not round dirty price to six decimal places.
Source literature comparisons explicitly apply the source's printed display
trace. Its illustrative six-place dirty stage is not inserted into continuous
analytics or inverted as a discrete price function. A caller supplying a
rounded auction price supplies price evidence for the cash operation.

## D4. Use the fractional-simple prefix and integer powers

For every raw bundle amount `A_j`, define the ideal model:

```text
alpha = r/s; b = 1+y/2; q = 1+alpha*y/2
V_j = A_j / (q*b^j); D_ideal = sum V_j; y finite and y > -2.
```

This covers the seven Appendix B forms, including the absent long-first
j=0 payment and its combined source coefficient at j=1. It is not the gilt
fractional-power convention. Negative yields and broad C are local model
extensions. The zero-yield path is finite and requires no annuity division
by zero.

## D5. Prefer a deterministic bounded rational observation

Treasury whole powers are rational. The current preferred kernel is therefore
small fixed-width integer outward arithmetic, rather than `powf` or a newly
assumed elementary-function profile. Use p=256 normalized significant bits
with a separately retained checked integer exponent. Convert **exact** b and
q from represented y and integer r/s; do not first round y/2 or alpha.
Use left-to-right binary powers, then the forward product `q*b^j`, then its
reciprocal. Every operation rounds low down and high up.

For `u=2^-255` and conversion/operation weights `(c,o)`, the research proves
`hi/lo <= 1/(1-N*u)` for `N=c+2o` and `N*u<1`. A factor cell has a safe
budget `N <= 3*j+4`; at `j<=19999` this is at most 60001. Error grows with
**j**, not merely the number of binary exponentiation operations.

The proposed deterministic observation is one unbounded-exponent 53-bit
nearest/ties-even rounding of the **lower endpoint**. It does not claim the
correct rounding of the ideal factor. With `t=2^-53` and `v=N*u`, it satisfies

```text
(1-t)*(1-v) <= factor_hat / factor_ideal <= 1+t.
```

This choice has a uniformly bounded tiny downward enclosure contribution and
always defines an observation, including near ideal rounding midpoints.
It avoids an unproved promise that p=256 always decides ideal nearest rounding.
The existing research checker compares both enclosure endpoints; a separate
observation oracle is required for this newly proposed selection rule.

Multiply each **exact** nonzero source amount by this extended factor and
round directly once to IEEE binary64. Reject infinity or positive-to-zero
PV, in original source index order. Do not first round the product to
extended53 and then round again into subnormals. Zero source coefficients
have exact-zero PV after their factor is observed. Later terms never rescue
a failed PV. Then reduce the accepted finite PVs exactly and round once via
the existing deterministic reduction contract.

This admission is also a resource decision: arbitrarily tiny extended PVs
are not retained in a giant exact sum. At the yield/j cap their exponent span
could exceed twenty million bits. Accepted **binary64** PVs permit bounded
existing exact reduction and bounded extended risk sums.

The final numeric contract must include exact operation algorithms, every
temporary width, factor exponent bounds, a direct IEEE PV proof, and absolute
subnormal error terms. The normal-PV two-rounding bound alone does not cover
subnormal relative error. No settlement enclosure producer is implied.

## D6. Define all six risks from the ideal model

For `delta=2+y` and each ideal V:

```text
eta_j = j/delta + alpha/(2*q)
V'_j = -V_j*eta_j
V''_j = V_j*[j*(j+1)/delta^2 + j*alpha/(delta*q) + alpha^2/(2*q^2)]
tau_j = (j+alpha)/2; M = sum(tau_j*V_j)
Modified = -D'/D; Macaulay = M/D; Convexity = D''/D.
```

The six getters are D', D'', M, Modified, Macaulay and Convexity, with
independent `Result` admission. Convexity has no half. These approximate
ideal-model derivatives, not derivatives of the rounding program. In a stub,
time-weighted Macaulay differs from `b*Modified`: at alpha=1/2, j=0, y=1/20
and A=205/2 they are 1/4 and 41/162 respectively.

Use each accepted IEEE PV and one exact-input nearest observation each of
delta and q. Form each complete derivative rational and round it once to
extended53; do not round the three convexity summands separately. Retain
fixed-sign extended sums and normalize directly by observed positive dirty.
A raw getter's range failure must not preempt an available normalized getter.
The contemplated 243-word, 2^-8192 basis needs the Treasury-specific proof;
it is not justified by quoting gilt's different coefficients.

The coupled q/delta premise must be proved from the **same** y/r/s. Independent
arbitrary private q/delta states do not represent public inputs. Carry,
aligned-sum, normalization and getter bounds all need explicit review.

## D7. Invert the observed price with explicit mathematical qualifications

Reuse bounded Brent's exact callback order, endpoint behavior, count limits,
residual scaling and cached successful callback payload. Targets are native
per100 `BondPrice`; clean targets shift once by cached published AI. Reject
nonpositive resolved dirty targets before callbacks. Never add a hidden
repricing, automatic bracket expansion or uncounted closed-form root.

The ideal positive model is strictly decreasing. Its binary observation is a
staircase and must not be described as a continuous strictly decreasing
function. Root reports retain residual and conditioning evidence; solver
coordinate convergence alone is not a financial accuracy certificate.

When all retained payments have j=0 and alpha<1, the ideal price has the
unattained supremum `sum(A_j)/(1-alpha)` as y approaches -2. Strict decrease
does not imply every positive target is attainable in that ideal model.
However, this is **not** an exact range bound for the observed price: factor
and PV rounding can reach or exceed that ideal supremum. Therefore do not
pre-reject a positive target using the ideal upper image or add an
unattainable-target error on that basis. With a retained j>=1 principal, the
ideal image is unbounded above. In all cases the caller's finite bracket and
actual binary callbacks determine whether solving succeeds.

A valid final-period discriminator has C=5, r=1, s=182 and
`y=next_up(-2)`. The ideal supremum is `18655/181`; the proposed p256
lower-endpoint factor and directly rounded IEEE PV give
`0x1.9c43e3b673fa6p+6`, **above** it by `47/6368371348078592`.
An ideal-image precheck would wrongly reject a price returned by evaluation.
This independently reproduced counterexample supersedes the earlier draft
precheck proposal. Its dates and exact selection steps are retained at
`/private/tmp/sabiquant-treasury-root-review-v1/ideal-supremum-counterexample.json`
(SHA-256 `e79835079629033aa085fcaad986a85987a62610f05de69fc20d5cbed2eba4f9`).

## D8. Keep four exact-money operations and their evidence

Provide coupon cash, published accrued cash, clean-price-plus-published-AI
invoice and dirty-price trade payment together. Nominal is strictly positive
exact par; final cash rounding and invoice aggregation are explicit existing
types. Price evidence remains `SettlementEvidence`, with its supplied-value
or declared-enclosure provenance. Analytics never manufacture an ideal-price
enclosure or strengthen a caller's guarantee.

Coupon cash scales the selected-basis coupon before its final cash quantizer.
Accrued cash uses the five-place per-$1,000 published stage before nominal
scaling. Invoice aggregation must name its capital, source AI and final
stages; it must not repeat source publication rounding or use raw AI instead.
Dirty cash performs one final cash quantizer and no accrued shift. Source
publication stages always use the retained Treasury tie rule; final cash
uses the caller's independent `SettlementRounding`.

Keep rational intermediates wide until the requested final DecimalAmount.
Do not introduce intermediate i128 or nearest-float gates. Compact owned
instructions retain dates, source ratios, C, source policy, nominal, evidence
and all cash policies. Equal numerical money does not erase differing
definition/provenance identity. The complete contract must specify Exact
semantics, endpoint ambiguity, stage-error precedence, stage strings and
full-input-space width proofs, not just copy gilt's stage names.

## D9. Bound preparation and preserve error meaning

Propose 20,000 quasi dates and 20,000 bundles, explicit caller caps, counted
before allocation. Source and analytical preparation reserve fallibly once
per owned vector; factor and derivative work use fixed small temporaries.
All numeric indices refer to original absolute quasi indices; coupon-cash
indices refer to actual coupon ordinals. Error causes preserve existing
Date/Amount/Sum/Value/Solver errors; internal proof-excluded capacity failures
must not be mislabeled as financial rate-domain failures.

Instrument identity retains original D/I, supplied Q, F/M, EOM, C and source
policy. Reports retain sufficient owned scalar context; they do not clone
the full instrument. Fallible construction does not promise that ordinary
Clone/Debug can never allocate. Add no defaults, implicit float-money
conversion or new hidden policy registry.
